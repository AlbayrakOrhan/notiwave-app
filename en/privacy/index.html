1. Who we are

NotiWave is a mobile app that lets you send custom sound-and-image notifications to your friends. It is developed and operated individually by Orhan Albayrak (İstanbul, Türkiye). This policy explains how we collect, use and protect your personal data.

Orhan Albayrak is the data controller for your personal data. Contact: albayrakorhann@gmail.com

2. What data we collect

We only collect what is needed to provide the service:

  • Account information: full name, email address, username and password. Your password is stored securely by Google Firebase Authentication; we cannot see it.
  • Profile: an optional profile photo.
  • Social connections: friend requests, your friend list and users you block.
  • Content: the title, message, image, chosen sound, recipients and schedule of notifications you send; recipients' reactions and delivery statuses; templates you save.
  • Device and notification data: your device's push notification token (Expo Push Token). Language and theme preferences are stored only on your device.
  • Purchase data: the product you bought, transaction ID, date and your credit balance. Your card and payment details are processed by Apple or Google and never reach us.
  • Technical logs: server logs (IP address, request time, error information) for security and debugging.

3. What we do not collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, microphone or advertising ID. The app contains no advertising, behavioral analytics or cross-app tracking. If this changes, we will update this policy and let you know.

4. Why we use your data

  • To create and manage your account and provide the service (performance of a contract).
  • To deliver your notifications to recipients and send scheduled notifications.
  • To process purchases and credits.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and spam, and review reports (legitimate interest).
  • To comply with legal obligations.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only as needed for the service to work:

  • Recipients: the content of a notification you send, your name and (depending on your privacy settings) your profile photo are visible to the recipient. Friends see your profile to the extent your privacy settings allow.
  • Google Firebase: account authentication.
  • Cloudinary: storing and serving images you upload.
  • Expo, Apple (APNs) and Google (FCM) notification services: delivering push notifications.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play: payments and subscriptions.
  • Our hosting provider: server infrastructure.
  • Public authorities: if required by applicable law.

6. International transfers

The service providers above may operate servers outside Türkiye (for example in the United States and Europe), so your data may be transferred internationally. Transfers are carried out in line with Article 9 of Turkish Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data and, where applicable, other data protection laws, with the security measures those providers apply.

7. Retention

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete your account in the app (Profile > Privacy > Delete My Account and Data): your profile information (name, email, username, photo link), friendships and requests, block list, templates, privacy and notification settings, push token and scheduled notifications that have not been sent yet are deleted; your account is anonymized and your Firebase identity account is deleted. Notifications you sent earlier keep appearing in recipients' history under the name "Deleted User" with your identity removed, so they can no longer be linked to you. Data we must keep by law, such as purchase records, may be kept anonymously for the required period. To request removal of image files stored on Cloudinary, write to albayrakorhann@gmail.com. Copies in technical backups are destroyed within a reasonable time (no later than 30 days).

8. Your privacy controls

In Profile > Privacy you can manage whether you receive friend requests, who can see your profile and profile photo, who can see your friend list, whether you appear in people search and which notifications you receive. You can block any user and report inappropriate content.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have rights such as access, correction, deletion, objection to processing, data portability and withdrawal of consent. Under Turkish Law No. 6698 (Article 11) you can learn whether your data is processed, request information, request correction or deletion, object to processing and claim compensation for damages. You can send requests to albayrakorhann@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days. You can delete your account and data at any time from within the app.

10. Children

NotiWave is not intended for people under 13. If we learn that someone under 13 created an account, we will delete the account and its data. Please tell us if you become aware of this.

11. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data (encrypted communication, authorization checks, secure use of the authentication service). However, no system is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will announce important changes in the app. The current version is always available in the app and on the web page.
