Data controller

Under Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK"), your personal data is processed by Orhan Albayrak (İstanbul, Türkiye) as data controller, as described below. Contact: albayrakorhann@gmail.com

Personal data processed

  • Identity and contact: full name, email address, username.
  • Visual: optional profile photo and images in notifications you send.
  • Transaction: purchase records, transaction ID, credit balance.
  • Transaction security: IP address, server logs, device notification token.
  • User content: notification title, message, preferred sound, recipients, friendship and blocking information.

Purposes

  • Creating your account and providing the service.
  • Delivering notifications to recipients and sending scheduled notifications.
  • Processing purchases and credits.
  • Keeping the service secure, preventing abuse and unlawful content, reviewing reports.
  • Fulfilling legal obligations.

Legal grounds

Your data is processed under KVKK Article 5/2: processing is directly related to the formation or performance of a contract, necessary for the controller to fulfil a legal obligation, and necessary for the controller's legitimate interests provided your fundamental rights and freedoms are not harmed.

Recipients and purposes of transfer

Your data may be transferred to recipients (to deliver notification content), Google Firebase (authentication), Cloudinary (image storage), Expo/Apple/Google (push delivery), Apple/Google (payments and subscriptions), our hosting provider, and competent public authorities where required by law. Because these providers' servers may be located abroad, your data may be transferred abroad in line with KVKK Article 9.

Method of collection

Your data is collected electronically, by automatic and partially automatic means, through the mobile app during registration, use and purchase.

Your rights under KVKK Article 11

By applying to the controller you may request to:

  • learn whether your personal data is processed,
  • request information if it has been processed,
  • learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly,
  • know the third parties it is transferred to, in Türkiye or abroad,
  • request correction if incomplete or inaccurate,
  • request deletion or destruction under KVKK Article 7,
  • request that these actions be notified to third parties the data was transferred to,
  • object to an outcome against you resulting from exclusively automated analysis,
  • claim compensation if you suffer damage from unlawful processing.

How to apply

Send your requests to albayrakorhann@gmail.com with information that lets us verify your identity. Requests are concluded within 30 days at most, depending on their nature. You can also delete your account and data from within the app (Profile > Privacy > Delete My Account and Data). If you believe your rights have been violated, you may also complain to the Personal Data Protection Board.
