Account and Data Deletion

Effective date: October 15, 2026

HomeTürkçe

1. Delete inside the app (fastest)

While signed in to NotiWave: go to Profile > Privacy > Delete My Account and Data and confirm with your password. Deletion happens immediately.

2. Request deletion without the app

If you cannot access the app or have uninstalled it, email albayrakorhann@gmail.com from the email address registered to your account with the subject "Account deletion request". To verify your identity, write from the registered address or include your account username. We complete requests within 30 days at the latest.

3. Data that is deleted

  • Your profile information: name, email, username, photo link
  • Your friendships, incoming/outgoing requests and block list
  • Your templates, message lists, privacy and notification settings
  • Your device notification token and any scheduled notifications not yet sent
  • Your Firebase identity account (your sign-in credentials)

4. Data that is retained or remains

Notifications you sent earlier remain in recipients' history under the name "Deleted User", with your identity removed; they cannot be linked to you. Data subject to legal retention obligations, such as purchase records, may be kept anonymously for the required period. Copies in technical backups are destroyed within 30 days at the latest. To have images stored on Cloudinary removed, write to albayrakorhann@gmail.com.

5. Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel your App Store or Google Play subscription. Please cancel it separately in your store account settings.
